Problem Statement:
    Farmers often struggle to identify crop diseases at an early stage due to limited access to agricultural experts and proper guidance. Delayed disease detection can lead to crop damage, reduced yield, and financial losses.
  The major challenges include:
      Difficulty in identifying crop diseases.
      Limited access to agricultural experts.
      Delayed detection of diseases.
      Increased crop damage and financial losses.
Solution Overview :
     Our project is an AI-powered crop disease detection system that analyzes crop leaf images to identify possible diseases and provides suitable treatment recommendations.
  How It Works
    1.Capture / Upload Leaf Image
    2.AI analyzes the image
    3.Disease is detected
    4.Treatment recommendation is provided
    5.Farmer takes timely action
The system helps farmers detect diseases early and make faster decisions for effective crop management.
